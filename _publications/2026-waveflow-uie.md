WaveFlow-UIE is a single-pipeline flow model for underwater image enhancement that works in the wavelet domain and includes a physics-prior branch. It runs inference about 4× faster than a diffusion baseline and improves perceptual metrics across multiple underwater datasets.

This work was done with Dr. Muzammil Behzad at KFUPM.
